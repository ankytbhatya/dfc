01
What is the DFC?

The Dedicated Freight Corridor is a network of high-capacity railway lines built exclusively for freight traffic across India, reducing congestion on passenger rail and significantly cutting logistics costs for industry.

02
District Coverage

This dataset maps all 641 Indian districts against the DFC network using 2011 Census boundaries, classifying each district as a DFC district, a border district, or outside the corridor entirely.

03
Query with AI

The interactive map includes a natural language interface powered by Claude. Ask questions like "show DFC districts in Gujarat" or "which border districts are in Rajasthan" and see results highlighted instantly.
